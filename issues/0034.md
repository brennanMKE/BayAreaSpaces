## Description

Contact each space with the ask that fits it. Contacts, named staff and per-space asks are in `spaces/README.md`; full tables with sources and caveats are in each `spaces/*.md`.

## Expected behavior

Work the ask ladder — rung 1 is a better pitch than rung 2 because it is not a favor to us:

1. **Publish a SpaceAPI endpoint** with `feeds.calendar` — one small JSON file, joins a 249-space standard, benefits every aggregator, and makes the space's registry entry self-maintaining.
2. **Give us a stable ICS URL.**
3. **Let us keep parsing what you publish** — and tell us what to exclude.

Priority order:

- **Hacker Dojo** — `info@hackerdojo.com` (note `.com`), attn Qi Diaz. Their events app has 525'd for over a year and they cross-post by hand. Strongest pitch we have.
- **Humanmade** — Amber Anderson cc Sandra Spurlock. Cheapest ask: they already run a public Luma calendar, just put workshops on it. Free favor: their Linktree button points at a domain that fails DNS.
- **Frontier Tower** — `events@frontiertower.io`. Ask for a read-only key to `events:listLocations`; it would end the location-string guesswork permanently.
