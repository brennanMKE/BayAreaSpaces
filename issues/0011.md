## Description

Produce the merged calendar — the artifact that makes every later decision easier to judge, and the demo that makes outreach persuasive.

## Expected behavior

- One `VCALENDAR`, `PRODID:-//brennan.sstools.co//maker-calendar//EN`, `X-WR-CALNAME:Bay Area Makerspaces`, `X-WR-TIMEZONE:America/Los_Angeles`.
- **Emit expanded instances, not RRULEs.** Cross-client recurrence handling is where this goes wrong, and expansion is already in memory.
- Every `VEVENT` carries `URL` pointing at the space's own page and `ORGANIZER` naming the space. The point is to drive people to the spaces.
- Prefix `SUMMARY` with the space: `[Ace] Sewing 101 Bootcamp`.
- `DESCRIPTION` truncated to roughly 300 characters plus a link — sidesteps the copyright question and reads better merged.
- Also emit per-space files under `out/spaces/{space_id}.ics`.
- **Validate before replacing the live file**: round-trip through `icalendar`, assert it parses and the event count is within tolerance.

## Actual behavior

Nothing emitted.
