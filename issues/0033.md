## Description

**The highest-leverage action in the whole project, and it is not code.** The Omni Commons house calendar is an Airtable embed, and Airtable supports native iCal sync. One toggle on their end yields a Tier-A feed covering every Omni collective.

## Expected behavior

Email `commons@lists.omnicommons.org` — the Commons Working Group that maintains the building's calendar and event request form, meeting 2nd & 4th Mondays at 5pm — asking them to enable iCal sync on the "Public Calendar" view (`shrxXjo869sxBzHmr`, view `viwJFaj6nFsS1poak`).

## Notes

That single feed would cover Sudo Room, **Counter Culture Labs** (which has no feed at all today and is otherwise Meetup-only), Liberated Lens, Food Not Bombs and the rest of the collectives.

The Airtable data endpoints are walled — `readSharedViewData` 302s to login, `downloadCsv` 403s behind PerimeterX — so there is no engineering route. This is a request, not code.

Pair it with the Sudo Room asks (issue 0034), since the two overlap in people and venue.

Lead with the working calendar once Phase 1 ships. Showing beats asking.
