## Description

Parse `<script id="__NEXT_DATA__">` from Eventbrite organizer pages. Eventbrite has no usable public search API since 2019 and **no longer emits JSON-LD** on organizer profiles — they moved to Next.js.

## Expected behavior

- Extract `props.pageProps.upcomingEvents[]` and `props.pageProps.upcomingEventsTotal`.
- Map `start_date` + `start_time` + `timezone`, plus `primary_venue.address` where present.
- When `upcomingEventsTotal` is 0, return empty cleanly — do not error, and do not let it trip the zero gate (see `require_nonzero_once`, issue 0016).
- Honor the rate limit. Eventbrite's ToS restricts scraping; these are published organizer pages at low volume, and the preferred long-term answer is to ask the space to publish elsewhere.

## Notes

**This adapter exists because of a correction.** Humanmade's Eventbrite source was registered as `jsonld`; since Eventbrite's migration that would have returned empty forever without ever erroring. A wrong adapter is as silent as a wrong URL.

Registered consumers and their state on 2026-08-05:

- The Crucible — **7 events**, all "Free Crucible Tour". The only source for their free public tours and the only one carrying a location.
- Humanmade — `upcomingEventsTotal: 0`, confirmed three ways.
- The Box Shop — 0, `enabled: false`.
- Lower 48 — 0, commented out entirely.

A Wayback snapshot of Humanmade's page from 2025-11 *does* have JSON-LD, but as `ItemList`/`ListItem` rather than top-level `Event` — so even the historical assumption needed adjusting.

## Root cause

Not a bug — a missing adapter, and a correction. Eventbrite migrated organizer profiles to a Next.js app (`/organizer-profile/_next/…`), so `https://www.eventbrite.com/o/…` carries **zero** `application/ld+json` blocks and the events live only in `<script id="__NEXT_DATA__" type="application/json">` at `props.pageProps.upcomingEvents`. Humanmade's source had been registered as `jsonld` against that URL, which would have returned an empty list every night, forever, without ever erroring.

Two things about the payload do not fit any existing adapter: a start arrives as **three separate fields** (`start_date` + `start_time` + `timezone`), and `primary_venue.address` is the only real location anywhere in the registry for The Crucible — its course-catalog blob and its Store API have no location field at all.

## Fix

`pipeline/adapters/nextdata.py`, built on issue 0021's `embedded_json` exactly as that agent designed for. `EVENTBRITE_ORGANIZER_FIELD_MAP` is an `embedded_json` `FieldMap` with `items="props.pageProps.upcomingEvents"` and `start_format="iso"`; the item walk, the `{id}:{start_epoch}` UID rule, the horizon clip, the counting and the text/money helpers are **imported**, not reimplemented. `script_id` is hardcoded to `__NEXT_DATA__`, which is the difference between this adapter and `embedded_json`; a source needing another blob name is an `embedded_json` source.

Only two pieces of real code are Eventbrite's:

- **`assemble_start`** joins `start_date` and `start_time` into one ISO wall clock, parsed against the item's **own** `timezone`. `tz` then carries that IANA name rather than the module's Pacific default, so the fixture's New York panel converts at `-04:00` while the Oakland tours convert at `-07:00` before 2026-11-01 and `-08:00` after it. An unresolvable timezone falls back to `America/Los_Angeles` and is **counted** (`assumed_timezone_count`), never assumed silently. A `start_date` with no `start_time` is undated and counted, not midnight.
- **`venue_address`** reads `primary_venue.address` in all four forms Eventbrite writes it (bare string, `localized_address_display`, the multi-line list, the address parts), falling back to the venue name. The survey recorded the value and not the sub-key, and guessing one form would publish `None` for the others — silent-empty, one field down.

**Zero is an observation, and only when the page declares it.** `upcomingEvents: []` **and** `upcomingEventsTotal: 0` returns `ok`, `parsed` and `reported_zero` with no events and no error status; issue 0016's `require_nonzero_once` takes it from there. Every other way of looking empty is its own `NextDataProblem`: `BLOB_NOT_FOUND`, `NOT_JSON`, `NO_PAGE_PROPS`, `NO_EVENTS_KEY` (the key was renamed), `NO_TOTAL` (no total, so a real zero and a renamed field are indistinguishable and the adapter refuses to guess in favour of zero), `COUNT_DISAGREES` (in both directions) and `NO_DATES`.

**One rate-limited GET per source, ever.** No `?tab=past` (it answers with the same blob; past events are client-fetched), no page walk, and no guess at the XHR behind `hasMoreUpcoming` — that flag is reported as a note instead, because inventing an undocumented internal endpoint is what Eventbrite's terms are aimed at. The standing recommendation in `spaces/humanmade.md` and `spaces/lower-48.md` is unchanged: ask the space to publish elsewhere.

Wired into `pipeline/cli.py` with neither `paginates` nor `needs_source` — it reads one page and takes no registry configuration.

## Gotchas

- **`embedded_json._build_events` hardcodes `tz` to its own module default.** That is right for its consumer and wrong for an organizer whose event is in another timezone, so `_walk` re-stamps `tz`, `source_tz` and `dtstart_form` on the events each item produced. If `embedded_json` ever grows a per-item zone, delete that step rather than leaving two places that decide the zone.
- **`upcomingEventsTotal` is load-bearing, not decoration.** It is the only thing separating "this organizer has nothing scheduled" from "the key we read was renamed". If Eventbrite drops the field, this adapter goes to `NO_TOTAL` and fails loudly rather than publishing zero — which is the intended behavior, not a regression to patch out.
- **`hasMoreUpcoming: true` means we are missing events on purpose.** It was `false` for every consumer on 2026-08-05. If it starts coming back true, the answer is to ask the space for a real feed, not to reverse-engineer the pagination XHR.
- **`price` and `categories` are deliberately unmapped.** The organizer payload has price-ish and tag-ish fields; the survey pinned neither, and a free tour showing a wrong price is worse than one showing none.
- **Collection pages (`/cc/<slug>`) have no `__NEXT_DATA__` at all** — 370 KB and nothing to read, recorded in `spaces/the-crucible.md`. Pointing this adapter at one gives `BLOB_NOT_FOUND`, which is correct and is not a reason to loosen the check.
- Individual Eventbrite **event** pages still emit clean `EducationEvent` JSON-LD and belong to `jsonld` (issue 0020). Both facts are true at once and confusing them costs a space in either direction.
