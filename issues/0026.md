## Description

Noisebridge's ~34-row standing weekly schedule (Circuit Hacking Monday, Woodhacking, Five Minutes of Fame, Gamebridge…) exists **only** on their wiki. Their Luma feed has just 1 event at or after today, so without this the space is effectively absent from the calendar.

The plan is to extract once, convert to hand-maintained RRULEs in the registry, and stop crawling — rather than run a model over a wiki page nightly.

## Expected behavior

The standing schedule is encoded as RRULEs in a maintainable form, and `Special:RecentChanges?feed=rss` is watched to know when to revisit.

## Actual behavior

Captured as a table in `spaces/noisebridge.md`, not yet encoded.

## Notes

**Four ambiguities need a human before encoding — do not guess:**

1. Sunday 3D Printing Class is "every other week" with **no anchor date** on the wiki. Needs a known-good date to fix the phase.
2. School of Melee: row tags say 1st & 3rd Friday, the prose description says 1st & 2nd. Self-contradictory.
3. Several Friday rows give end times as `11:30am` / `11:59am` where `pm` is obviously meant.
4. "Hack on Noisebridge!" is flagged pink on the wiki, meaning "caution maybe dead". Verify before publishing.

Deliberately excluded: TRASH NIGHT (a space chore, not a public event) and the Handsewing Workshop (on-request only, not scheduled).

Best route for all four: the **Tuesday 7pm weekly consensus meeting**, which is also where a calendar proposal has to land (issue 0031). Discord is faster for reaching individual event hosts.

Source is the MediaWiki API wikitext, not the rendered page: `api.php?action=parse&page=Category:Events&prop=wikitext`. It carries explicit `{{Recurring}}` / `{{RecurringNumbered|1st}}` markers. Note `/wiki/Events` is only a redirect, and their robots.txt fully disallows ClaudeBot and Amazonbot.
